## Overall Impression

This is a credible, content-led institutional site that mostly earns its "Governing Record" positioning — the homepage's hand-built NeuralGlobe animation and the specificity of the board/project copy are genuine strengths. But two things undercut it: the site's own Get Involved / Contact split has collapsed into a confusing duplicate, and the site doesn't yet manage the density of its own content (Mission's wall of text, Charter's ungrouped governance dump). The single biggest opportunity is fixing the `/contact` vs `/get-involved` overlap — it's currently the weakest link at the exact moment (deciding to join) the site most needs to convert.

## What's Working

- **NeuralGlobe hero animation** (`components/NeuralGlobe.tsx`) — a genuine, hand-built signature element (not stock imagery, not a Lottie template) that backs up the "credible, technical" brand personality with real craft, including the documented `cssColorToRgb()` safeguard against silent color-parsing failures.
- **Specificity discipline in content** — board bios (`data/board.ts`) and project descriptions (`data/projects.ts`) consistently name real institutions, real people, and real numbers rather than falling back to generic marketing language, exactly matching PRODUCT.md's "specificity over hype" principle.
- **Consistent card elevation system** — `.card-surface` / `.card-surface-hover` (`app/globals.css:90-113`) is applied uniformly across Board, Project, Workstreams, and Get Involved cards, giving the site real cross-page visual consistency (confirmed clean by the deterministic scan too).

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Duplicate page identity between `/contact` and `/get-involved`**
- **Why it matters**: Both pages render the identical eyebrow+H1 "Get Involved" (`app/contact/page.tsx:18`, `app/get-involved/page.tsx:38`) with near-duplicate metadata titles. A user clicking the Nav's "Contact" pill lands on a page visually claiming to be a different page than the one they clicked — this breaks trust in navigation and is a genuine regression from when `/contact` had its own identity and form.
- **Fix**: Give `/contact` its own distinct header/title, or formally merge the two routes and redirect one to the other so there's exactly one "how to get involved" destination.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify` (page identity/copy), then `/impeccable polish`

**[P1] Contact is a dead end for the highest-stakes action on the site**
- **Why it matters**: `app/contact/page.tsx:69-78` reduces "become a member organization" to a single mailto link to one named individual, with no form, no team alias, and no confirmation state. Orgs evaluating a multi-year consortium commitment expect more institutional weight than a cold personal email — and it directly contradicts the CTA promise on `app/get-involved/page.tsx:71-76` ("we'll point you to the right workstream").
- **Fix**: Reintroduce a lightweight form or a general alias with an expected-response-time note, and route org vs. individual inquiries differently.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden` (missing confirmation/error states), `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] Mission page is an unbroken wall of text**
- **Why it matters**: `app/mission/page.tsx:31-127` runs nine consecutive prose paragraphs with only two stylistic breaks (the pull-quotes) and no subheadings — this fails the chunking/scannability bar for a technical-but-busy evaluator trying to judge relevance quickly.
- **Fix**: Add 3-4 subheadings (e.g. "The Problem," "The Framework," "Why Species Data," "Environmental Responsibility") so readers can jump to what they care about.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`, `/impeccable typeset`

**[P2] Homepage StatRow contradicts the project's own anti-pattern rule**
- **Why it matters**: `components/StatRow.tsx` rendered at `app/page.tsx:69-75` is a hero-metric block (four value/label stat pairs in a grid) directly beneath the hero — the exact pattern PRODUCT.md names as an anti-reference (uor.foundation / askshepherd.ai style SaaS scaffolding).
- **Fix**: Fold the stats into surrounding prose or the LogoWall context, or drop the isolated number grid entirely.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable distill`, `/impeccable colorize` (if kept, integrate rather than isolate)

**[P3] Nav offers 8 primary choices with no grouping**
- **Why it matters**: `components/Nav.tsx:9-17` lists 7 top-level links plus a separate Contact pill (8 total), exceeding the ≤4-5 working-memory guideline for primary wayfinding, and it collapses into the same flat, ungrouped list on mobile.
- **Fix**: Group related links (e.g. an "About" cluster for Mission/Charter/Board) to reduce visible top-level choices.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

## Persona Red Flags

**Academic/engineer evaluator (project-specific persona, derived from PRODUCT.md's target user)**: Hits the Mission wall-of-text (`app/mission/page.tsx`) with no way to skim to "governance" or "technical scope" without reading all nine paragraphs, then reaches Contact expecting a formal member-org pathway and instead finds one person's email in an informal register ("As this is a volunteer organization...") — a mismatch with the "credible, technical" bar the site sets for itself.

**Casey (Distracted Mobile User)**: The Nav's 7-link + Contact-pill list collapses into a functional hamburger dropdown (`components/Nav.tsx:106-130`), but it's the same flat, ungrouped 8-item list as desktop — meaning mobile users scroll through all 8 stacked items before reaching page content.
