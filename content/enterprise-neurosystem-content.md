## Mission

The world of AI model development is evolving rapidly — thousands of AI models can be deployed
in a global network by a multinational corporation. The org addresses the benefits and
challenges of AI/ML by creating a unified framework integrating multiple AI instances across
networks.

Draws a biological metaphor: like human physiology, many functions are autonomic while others
require conscious analysis — compared to IT data centers.

> "The Enterprise Neurosystem is the final stage of this evolution. It will enable a deeper and
> more complete level of AI awareness and perception..."

> "Humanity must directly engage with AI, as the combined effect of human and machine
> deterministic capabilities is beyond the power of either one alone."

Climate is the priority application area where large-scale AI architecture can make the most
impact for the global good.

Closing: **"Our consortium is a community, welcome to all."**

## Charter

### Project Objectives and Scope

AI/ML work tends to be organization/function-specific (narrow, proprietary datasets). Need for a
community effort to:

- Open up and standardize development of models and adaptive frameworks, using and creating public datasets
- Tie together various models and data sources across the enterprise in a unified framework
- Abstract prediction and forecasting techniques applicable across business domains
- Eventually apply AI/ML to wide-scale system analysis via cross-correlation across domains

Applied first to a defined set of open models/practice areas (network analysis, predictive
HW/SW maintenance, etc.) to test concepts and models at global scale. Also focused on expanding
access to the value of AI across enterprises by improving simplicity and usability.

### Technical Committee (TC)

Responsible for all aspects of oversight relating to coordinating the technical direction and
approving proposals per established processes. Coordinates community engagement, works with
external open source communities, establishes norms and security policies.

A Technical Community Leader (TCL) drives development and facilitates contributions. The
evaluation body was constituted with diversity considerations given AI's potential for encoding
bias. TCLs are nominated or self-nominated from among existing Committers and elected by
plurality vote from community members with accepted contributions in the prior twelve months.

"A Contributor may become a Committer by a majority approval of existing Committers, and at need
may be removed by majority approval of the other existing Committers."

Technical participation remains open to anyone following charter terms. "Technical Committee
meetings are conducted in English and are open to the public and documented for subsequent
review." Each sub-project ideally has at least two Committers with diversity emphasis.

### Governing Board

Sets strategic direction per the Mission and oversees budget, policies, legal matters, and
marketing.

Composition: a Chair, Vice Chair-Treasurer (combined role), and Technical Community
Leader/CTO. The Chair and Vice Chair-Treasurer cannot be from the same organization, and no more
than two board members may be from the same organization including subsidiaries. Each board seat
has a four year term.

"The Board may choose to admit additional members, such as Sector or Vertical representatives or
a User Community representative, on either a voting or non-voting basis." Total board composition
shall not exceed thirteen members. The Board may form operational workgroups for marketing,
finance and similar functions.

**Chair responsibilities:** organize/lead regular Board meetings; authorize payments per
Board-approved budgets; lead member company recruitment; regularly attend Technical Committee
meetings and align with the TCL.

**Vice Chair-Treasurer responsibilities:** develop annual operating plans and budgets; advise on
and validate operating plan budgets; review monthly payments authorized by the Director; report
regularly on revenues and expenditures relative to budget.

Industry sectors (Education/Research, Financial Services, IT Vendor, etc.) are created over
time; sector representatives elected by sector participants.

**Voting:** one vote per Governing Board Voter; additional voting members admitted by majority
vote; voters missing 3 consecutive meetings without proxy have voting capacity suspended;
missing 4+ may be removed and replaced; elections/decisions require 50% quorum; charter or
policy amendments require majority Board vote.

### Community Assets and Intellectual Property

"The Platform is licensed under the Apache v2.0 license. Subsidiary Projects may choose other
permissive licenses" specified in technical charters. Documentation and marketing content are
published under Creative Commons CC BY license. The Project owns repositories, social media
accounts, and domain registrations.

"Title to all trade or service marks used by the Project ('Project Trademarks') is currently
held by Red Hat, Inc." Upon joining an open source foundation or fiscal conservancy, trademarks
transfer to that entity.

### Compliance with Policies and Licenses

Contributors must comply with Governing Board-adopted policies including: Contributor
requirements including Developer Certificate of Origin; Code of Conduct and complaint process;
Terms of Use; Trademark and Branding Policy.

## Workstreams

Initial focus: "a self-describing digital asset catalog, and an AI signal processing framework
and tools."

### 1. Self-describing Digital Asset Catalog

"This will be the core framework where the AI models reside and operate." The proposal involves
creating a catalog as a foundation, eventually supporting "a cross-correlation AI engine for
deeper pattern analysis."

Repository: https://github.com/Enterprise-Neurosystem/sd-catalog

### 2. AI Signal Processing

Concentrates on "tools and solutions that can process high-rate signal data for acoustics,
vibrations and other areas of analysis." Includes studying global bee populations to prevent
decline. Codebase originated from IBM Research as an open source contribution.

Repository: https://github.com/Enterprise-Neurosystem/ai-signal-processing

Engagement: interested contributors can "join the effort" through the community onboarding page.
