Below we are listing all of the prizes that the Grand Prize winner and 2 runner up finalists in our AI Innovation Grand Challenge will receive from competition sponsors.

For the most part, sponsorships are for in-kind donations meant to be directly useful to those developing and implementing AI-driven climate solutions.

The Grand Prize winner will benefit from sponsored attendance of COP 29, taking place in Baku, Azerbaijan in November where the winner of the competition will be formally announced.

### **Platinum Sponsors**

### Kove

Kove will award the top three teams $5 million in credits to use Kove:SDM™, its Software-Defined Memory technology.

“Kove is proud to contribute our Software-Defined Memory solution to the Enterprise Neurosystem for its AI Innovation Grand Challenge. By optimizing AI model performance and reducing energy consumption, our technology enables participants to develop sustainable AI-powered solutions that tackle the climate crisis head-on,” says [John Overton](https://www.linkedin.com/in/john-overton-693539/), CEO of Kove.

More at [https://kove.com/](https://kove.com/)

### Red Hat

Red Hat will award the top three teams $300,000 in credits for Red Hat OpenShift AI on AWS (ROSA), a fully managed cloud service that empowers organizations to rapidly build and deploy artificial intelligence and machine learning models.

“Red Hat is proud to support the next generation of climate innovators through the AI Innovation Grand Challenge. We believe that by fostering open collaboration and providing access to sustainable technologies, we can accelerate the development of AI solutions that address environmental concerns,” says Sandro Mazziotta, Director, Technology Strategy at Red Hat.

More @ [https://www.redhat.com/en/technologies/cloud-computing/openshift/openshift-ai](https://www.redhat.com/en/technologies/cloud-computing/openshift/openshift-ai)

### Amazon Web Services

Amazon Web Services (AWS) is awarding a total of $250,000 in AWS Service credits to the top three teams. The grand prize winner and two runners-up can use the AWS Services credits to access AWS’s comprehensive cloud computing platform, which offers a vast array of on-demand services including computing power, storage, databases, and more.

“AI holds immense promise for humanity, and our generation will decide how to harness it for good. We’re excited to sponsor the AI Innovation Grand Challenge, providing access to compute and storage to empower researchers and developers to build impactful AI solutions. I’m eager to see the winning innovations tackle the world’s to-do list,” says Sandra Topic, AWS AI for Good Lead.

More @ [https://aws.amazon.com/](https://aws.amazon.com/)

### **Silver Sponsors**

### Google

Google will award the Grand Prize winner a $40,000 package comprising a place in its Google Startups for Sustainable Development program for six months. This prize includes:

-   A Google Certificate recognizing their impact
-   Unlimited advice sessions from hundreds of Google volunteers with expertise in a variety of different domains
-   Dedicated Google Cloud credits

More @ [https://events.withgoogle.com/startups-for-sustainable-development/](https://events.withgoogle.com/startups-for-sustainable-development/)
