[Alisa Luangrath](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alisa-luangrath-a43638271/) and her team from Lao People’s Democratic Republic have won this year’s **AI for Climate Action Award**, following live pitches by the finalists to an international panel of judges, including Enterprise Neurosystem Founder and Chairman Bill Wright.

Alisa’s open-source multilingual solution dubbed Smart AI-based Farming & Irrigation for Resilience (SAFIR) is transforming the way farmers in her country’s Southern province of Savannakhet adapt to water scarcity & climate variability.

Through this solution, Alisa and her team are helping smallholder farmers strengthen resilience, boost productivity, and protect vital water resources while empowering local communities & government planners.

See this previous [blog post](https://medium.com/the-enterprise-neurosystem-blog/finalists-chosen-for-ai-for-climate-change-award-2025-d06acc551f6c) for more information on the finalists. The award is hosted by the UNFCCC TEC, the UNFCCC CTCN, and the Korea International Cooperation Agency (KOICA) in collaboration with the Enterprise Neurosystem.
