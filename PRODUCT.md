# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Researchers, engineers, academics, and technology companies evaluating whether to contribute to or join the Enterprise Neurosystem consortium — an open-source AI research community focused on climate change. Also includes government/NGO partners (e.g. UNFCCC) and press. Visitors arrive to understand the mission, see who governs it (Board), see what's being built (Projects, Workstreams), and find a path to participate (Get Involved, Charter, Press).

## Product Purpose

Inform and recruit. The site explains the Enterprise Neurosystem's mission (a unified global AI framework for climate data), its governance (Charter, Board), its technical output (Projects, Workstreams), and its news (Press), and converts interested readers — individuals or organizations — into contributors or members via the Get Involved page.

## Brand Personality

Credible, technical, mission-driven. This is a serious research-consortium tone: precise, substantive, and purposeful rather than hype-driven. Confidence should come from specificity (named institutions, named board members, named projects) rather than marketing language.

## Anti-references

- https://uor.foundation/
- https://askshepherd.ai/

Avoid generic SaaS-landing-page scaffolding (hero-metric blocks, gradient text, uniform icon+heading+text card grids, tiny uppercase eyebrows on every section) — this is an institutional/consortium site, not a product pitch.

## Design Principles

- Specificity over hype: named people, named projects, named institutions carry credibility more than adjectives.
- Content-led, not decoration-led: the mission and governance content is the product; layout should get out of its way.
- One visual language across all informational pages (Mission, Charter, Board, Projects, Workstreams, Press, Get Involved) so the site reads as one coherent institution, not a set of disconnected pages.
- Restraint: climate/AI urgency should come through in copy, not through aggressive visual noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA baseline: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1, visible focus states, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected throughout (already implemented via `Reveal` and `NeuralGlobe`).
